Draft Modes

Normal Draft

Standard competitive draft with bans and picks. Champions are available for selection in each game.

  • 5 bans per team
  • 10 picks (5 per team)
  • Alternating ban/pick order

Fearless Draft

Champions banned or picked in previous games cannot be selected again in the same series.

  • 5 bans per team
  • 10 picks (5 per team)
  • Cumulative champion pool restriction

Ironman Draft

Each player must pick a champion for their specific role. Adds strategic depth to drafting.

  • 5 bans per team
  • 10 picks (5 per team)
  • Position-specific drafting
